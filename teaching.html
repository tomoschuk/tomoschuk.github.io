Teaching

In graduate school I was a teaching assistant for statistics, experimental methods, and related courses. Since then, I have instructed the classes below at UC San Diego through Psychology and the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute (HDSI).

Instructor of Record (UC San Diego)

  • DSC 20 — Introduction to Python

    Introductory Python from basic types, control flow, and collections through comprehensions, recursion, and complexity, to classes, inheritance, and exceptions.

  • DSC 80 — Practice and Application of Data Science

    The data-science lifecycle in Python—pandas and tidy analysis, hypothesis and permutation testing, missing-data mechanisms and imputation, HTTP/HTML and web scraping, text features, and supervised modeling with pipelines, cross-validation, trees/forests, and classifier evaluation including fairness.

  • PSYC 60 — Introduction to Statistics

    Foundational statistics for the behavioral sciences—from descriptive statistics and probability through hypothesis tests, t-tests, ANOVA, correlation, regression, and chi-square.
