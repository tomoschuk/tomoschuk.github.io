I am a 5th year PhD student in the department of Experimental Psychology at UC San Diego, advised by Dr. Victor S. Ferreira and Dr. Tamar H. Gollan in the Language Production Lab. Generally, I study the language processing of bilinguals and second language learners.

I was also a Fulbright Scholar at Ghent University in Belgium, where I researched multilingualism and language learning in collaboration with Dr. Wouter Duyck and Dr. Robert Hartsuiker.

I did my undergraduate research using EEG (in particular ERPs) to study bilingual color perception in the BiLD lab advised by Dr. Janet van Hell and in collaboration with Dr. Guillaume Thierry at Bangor University in Wales.
